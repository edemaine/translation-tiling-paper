Coefficient 800 with two Wang decorations: complete
====================================================
The public Lean statements and texlish give

    (n+4)^(800 lg lg(n+4)) unit cubes, lg=log_2.

The same total computable function, Effective.pairCompile, has a nonempty,
face-connected output, both tilability directions, and this size bound.
It retains 2^250 for every input list of length at most 3. For length 4,
the finite compiler's explicit binary bound is 2^3770.

Construction and proof endpoints
---------------------------------
The nine-column arithmetic boards and their classification are unchanged.
PairBoard retains Wang decorations at columns 0 and 2. Shifted lines
m=M(1+2x) and m=M(1+3x) compare M with 5M and 7M respectively.
PairSamples proves canonical_shifted_coefficients and shifted_root_one
algebraically; no new SAT certificates are needed. PairBoardRecovery
proves row equality and both Wang adjacencies. PairBoardSoundness proves
the Wang-tilability equivalence for every tile set.

The 20-channel system has nine arity-4, nine arity-6, and two tile-label
channels, plus the same two singleton seeds. PairParameters and the six
PairProfile modules check prime separation, primality, positive digit
representations, support, cyclic order, mass, and geometric volume.
The safe binary exponents are 3770,3890,3950,4220,4980,5780 for the
ranges 4;5;6;7..10;11..20;21..30. The n=4 profile also optimizes the
useful-prime pairing. Its search is not trusted by the proof.

PairSmallCompiler checks the finite computable reductions. PairLogBound
and Effective/PairCompiler prove the uniform 800 bound. Inputs n>=31 use
the existing generic ternary compiler. Widths 64 and 80 cover 31..242
and 243..2043; for n>=2044 the bulk coefficient is 2248/3 <800.
Abstract.lean and AbstractBridge.lean expose the strengthened results.
The rotation bounds inherit 800 with the same 64000 factor in dimension 3.
The older 850 and 1000 compilers remain in the main axiom audit.

Reproduction
--------------
From the repository root (PYTHONUTF8=1 on Windows):
  python verification/generate_pair_prime_profiles.py
This reads verification/pair_prime_profiles.json and emits the six Lean
profile files; Lean checks every resulting numerical certificate.
From lean/:
  lake build
  lake build TranslationTiling.SudokuArithmetic.PairAudit
From the repository root:
  make all

The original mathematical notes and independent integer checks remain in
build/after850/, superseded by the complete Lean proofs described above.
