Nine-column arithmetic and coefficient 850: complete
====================================================
The public Lean statements, abstract bridge, and texlish now give

    (n+4)^(850 lg lg(n+4)) unit cubes, where lg=log_2.

The total computable function is FiniteCompiler.Effective.nineCompile.
Its facts theorem proves nonemptiness, face connectivity, both Wang/polycube
tilability directions, and the displayed size bound for the same output.
The bound 2^250 remains valid for every input list of length at most 3.

Construction and proof
-----------------------
Each arithmetic board retains {0,1,2,3,4,r,r+1,r+2,2r}, for r=5,7.
The three Wang decorations remain at 1,5,7 independently of arithmetic
column selection. There are 21 ordinary channels (9+9+3) and two seeds.

NinePatchFormula describes normalized nonzero patches with 9 columns and
7 rows, with primitive first-digit affine tests on integer slopes -6..6.
NinePatchKeys and NinePatchModel prove variable separation and semantic
satisfaction. The kernel checks the CNF identity and every LRAT inference.
The certificates have 12155/23294 initial clauses and 1888/13622 RUP
additions for primes 5/7 respectively. No solver is trusted by Lean.

NinePatchLocal proves normalized patch classification. NinePatchField
normalizes arbitrary nonzero field patches and removes normalization.
NinePatchOverlap proves 4x4 uniqueness algebraically and glues all integer
translates. NinePatchGlobal applies those results to unit-valued arrays.
NineArithmetic proves the full finite-depth classification; NineSamples
and NineBoardSoundness supply Wang-tile extraction and the equivalence.

NineParameters verifies the prime layout. The six NineProfile files
verify primality, uniqueness, positive representations, support, order,
mass, and geometric volume. The safe exponents in volume <= 2^e are:

    n range      e
    4           4010
    5           4100
    6           4200
    7..10       4580
    11..20      5670
    21..30      6810

NineSmallCompiler gives the computable reductions for these inputs.
The existing sparse ternary compiler handles n>=31. NineLogBound proves
the uniform coefficient 850, using fixed width profiles through n=1019
and a bulk estimate (12092/15) lg(n+4) lg lg(n+4) beyond that point.
Effective/NineCompiler combines all branches, including the empty input.
The earlier coefficient-1000 compiler remains checked by the main audit.
